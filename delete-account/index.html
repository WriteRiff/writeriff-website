Delete your WriteRiff account

WriteRiff is made by Beckman Studios LLC. You can delete your account at any time, from inside the app or by email.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open WriteRiff and sign in.
  2. Go to App Settings → Account.
  3. At the bottom, choose Delete account, read what will be deleted, type DELETE and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

Or ask us by email

Email support@writeriff.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my WriteRiff account". We will confirm the request with you and delete the account within 30 days, then email you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your WriteRiff account and sign-in (email address, password, display name).
  • Every Story copy saved to your account in the cloud, with its history, pictures and audiobook files.
  • Your account conversations and AI history, narration records and usage counts.

What is kept

  • Stories saved only on your device stay on your device. We can't reach them; delete them in the app or uninstall it if you want them gone.
  • A record that a deletion happened (an anonymous account number and the date, with no email or writing), and spending records for AI book covers and narration, which hold only an anonymous account number, dates and amounts. Sign-in security logs held by our hosting provider age out on its own schedule. We keep these to run the service safely and for accounting.

Questions: support@writeriff.com
