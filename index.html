Old soul, new pages

Everything a storyteller needs, finally in one place.

Writers have always gathered their tools around them: the notebook of ideas, the corkboard of scenes, the manuscript, the trusted reader with a red pen. WriteRiff brings all of it onto one beautiful desk, and lets you carry that desk in your pocket.

  • Plan, write, read and revise without juggling five different apps.
  • Your story, notes and critiques stay connected to each other.
  • Calm, clean and made for fiction, not for spreadsheets and memos.

What's on the desk

From first spark to final page.

Nine tools, one app, built around the way fiction actually gets written.

Idea Vault

Every stray thought, overheard line and what-if, kept safe and easy to find when you need it.

Story Structure

Build your entire story structure and plan in one spot. Customizable, clean and easy to use.

Folio

WriteRiff's own word processor, designed for fiction. Use it on its own or with the rest of the app.Prefer your favorite word processor? Bring your manuscript in and take it out as a Word document or plain text.

Write anywhere

Any browser on your computer, plus your phone and tablet. Never lose a flash of inspiration again.

Book reader

Read your manuscript just like a book you've bought, and share it with the people you choose.

Critiques

Request critiques from readers and writers, critique theirs, and keep it all organized, connected to your manuscript.

Annotate as you read

Mark the changes you want right on the page while you read, the way you would with a pencil.

Audiobooks

Turn your writing into an audiobook, listen back, and share it with friends and family.

AI, only if you want it

Never to write your novel. An optional helper to organize, untangle and spark ideas at every stage.

AI assistance, if you want it

You write the story. AI just helps you think.

WriteRiff's AI is an optional helper, never the author. Ask it to organize your notes, untangle a plot knot, question a character's motive or spark a fresh idea when you're stuck. Every word of your book stays yours.
