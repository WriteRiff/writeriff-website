Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

The short version. Your writing belongs to you. It's saved on your device, and copies go to your WriteRiff account only so you can sync and back them up. Text you send to the AI assistant, dictation or narration goes to the service that does that job, only when you use it. We don't use your writing to train AI, our AI provider (OpenAI) doesn't either, and we never sell your information. WriteRiff has no ads and no tracking.

This policy explains what information WriteRiff collects, why, who receives it, how long it's kept, and the choices and rights you have. It covers the WriteRiff apps for iPhone, iPad, Android and the web, and the writeriff.com website. During the beta, WriteRiff is offered only in the United States.

Who we are

WriteRiff is made by Beckman Studios LLC, an Arkansas limited liability company doing business as WriteRiff ("WriteRiff", "we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal information described here.

Questions, requests or complaints: support@writeriff.com.

What we collect

Your account

  • Email address and password. Used to create your account and sign you in. Your password is stored only in a protected (hashed) form by our hosting provider; we can't read it.
  • Display name, if you choose one.
  • An account number that our systems create to tell accounts apart.
  • Sign-in sessions. A sign-in token is kept on your device so you stay signed in: in the phone's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android), or in your browser's storage on the web. If you turn off "Stay signed in on this device", it's kept only until you close the app or tab.
  • Sign in with Google (when we offer it). If you choose it, Google shares your email address, name and Google account number with us (and a link to your Google profile picture, if you have one). We keep these with your account.

Your writing

  • Stories and everything in them: manuscripts, chapters, scenes, characters, places, notes, ideas, plans, versions and settings. These are saved on your device.
  • Cloud copies. When you're signed in and cloud sync is on, full copies of your Stories, their history and the records needed to sync them between devices are stored in your account.
  • Pictures you add (such as covers and story images) and audiobook files you generate, when you save them to your account.
  • AI conversations connected to your account's Home screen.

When you use AI, dictation or narration

  • AI assistant. Your message, recent messages in that conversation, and the parts of your Story the assistant needs to answer (for example the selected passage and relevant story facts).
  • AI book covers. The title, the author name if you add one, the style and colors you pick, and any description you type. OpenAI adds hidden information to the pictures it makes that identifies them as AI-generated (provenance metadata, also called C2PA). WriteRiff doesn't put a visible "AI-generated" mark on covers.
  • Dictation. Your voice, which is turned into text. Dictation usually uses your phone's or browser's own speech recognition (Apple, Google or your browser's maker; see below). The microphone in the AI assistant, and dictation on phones that can't do live recognition, send the recording to OpenAI instead.
  • Narration and audiobooks. The text being read aloud and the voice you chose. For narration you create, we keep a record of each request, including the text and the finished audio, so you aren't charged twice and can get the audio back.

Usage counts

To keep the service fair and protect it from runaway costs, we count how many AI, dictation and picture requests each account makes each day, and how much narration each account uses each month. For AI book covers we also record the time, the quality chosen, whether it worked and its estimated cost (never the title, description or picture).

Technical information

Our hosting provider and servers keep technical logs to keep WriteRiff running, secure it and fix problems: for example your IP address, your device or browser type, the time of a request, which feature was used and whether it worked. Sign-in security logs also record sign-ins and the email address used. Our server code is built not to write your manuscript text into these logs.

What we don't collect

  • No advertising identifiers, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no analytics or crash-reporting services in the app.
  • No payment card details (WriteRiff has no paid features today).
  • No contacts, location or photos beyond the pictures you choose to add. (A picture file can carry details your camera saved in it, such as where it was taken; WriteRiff doesn't read or use them.)

How we use it

We use your information only to:

  • Run WriteRiff for you: sign you in, save and sync your Stories, and provide the AI, dictation and narration features you ask for.
  • Keep it safe and fair: prevent abuse, enforce usage limits and keep your account secure.
  • Contact you about your account: confirm your email, reset your password, and tell you when your password or email changes.
  • Answer you when you write to us.
  • Meet legal obligations.

We don't send marketing emails today. If we add product news or update emails, we'll send them only if you say yes, and every one will have an unsubscribe link.

Your writing is yours

You own everything you write in WriteRiff. We store, sync and show it only to provide the service to you (see our Terms of Use). We don't read your Stories, publish them, sell them, or use them to train AI.

When you use an AI feature, what you send goes to OpenAI through our server so it can answer. OpenAI's terms for business customers say that data sent through its API is not used to train its models unless the customer opts in, and we haven't. For the AI assistant, we also ask OpenAI not to keep the conversation for its own use (we turn off its "store" setting). OpenAI may keep requests (including cover descriptions and the pictures it makes) for up to 30 days to watch for abuse, then deletes them, unless the law requires otherwise. OpenAI also checks requests with automated safety filters, and may refuse ones that break its rules.

Who receives your information

We use a small number of service providers to run WriteRiff. They process your information on our behalf and only for the job described here. We never sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

  • Supabase (account, database, storage and servers). Hosts your account, cloud copies, pictures, audiobook files and usage counts. Our data is stored in the United States (Ohio). Supabase encrypts stored data and all connections.
  • OpenAI (AI assistant, dictation, AI book covers). Receives the text or recording you send, or the cover's title, author name (if you add one) and description, through our server. Requests carry no email address or account details; the only name sent is an author or pen name you choose to put on an AI book cover.
  • Google Cloud Text-to-Speech (narration). Receives the text to be read and the voice name, through our server, and sends back audio. Google states that it doesn't log this text or audio, and its Cloud terms say it doesn't use customer data like this to train its AI models.
  • Your phone's or browser's speech recognition (dictation). On iPhone and Android, live dictation uses your phone's own speech recognition. Depending on your device, your voice may be processed on the device or sent to Apple or Google to turn it into text. On the web, your browser's speech recognition is used, and many browsers send the audio to their maker's servers (for example Google for Chrome, Microsoft for Edge, Apple for Safari). Their own privacy policies cover that processing. If your phone can't do live dictation, or it stops working, the recording goes to OpenAI instead, as above.
  • Email delivery (account emails). Our account emails are sent by our hosting provider's email service. We plan to move them to Zoho ZeptoMail (sending from accounts@writeriff.com), which would receive your email address and the message, and keeps sending logs for about 60 days. Account emails show our logo, loaded from writeriff.com, so opening one lets our website host see your IP address.
  • Google (Sign in with Google), only if we offer it and you choose it. Google keeps its own record that you signed in to WriteRiff.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or as part of a sale or merger of our business (in which case this policy would continue to protect your information).

We're testing other voice providers for narration. None of them receives your writing today; we'll update this policy before any of them does.

How long we keep it

  • Stories on your device: until you delete them or remove the app. We can't reach them.
  • Your account and cloud copies: until you delete them or delete your account. When you permanently delete a Story, its cloud copy is deleted too, except a small record that the Story was deleted (no writing) and its pictures, which stay in your account's picture library until you delete your account.
  • Pictures and audiobook files in your account: until you remove them or delete your account.
  • Narration records and usage counts: until you delete your account.
  • Technical and sign-in security logs: for the time our hosting provider sets, then deleted automatically (this also applies after you delete your account).
  • At OpenAI: up to 30 days for abuse monitoring, as described above.
  • After you delete your account we keep only: a record that the deletion happened and how far it got (your old account number and dates, with no email or writing), and spending records for AI book covers and narration, which hold only the old account number, dates and amounts. We keep these to run the service safely and for our accounts.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: App Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account is deleted right away.
  • By email: write to support@writeriff.com from the email address you sign in with. We'll confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days.
  • Full details: writeriff.com/delete-account.

Deleting your account removes your sign-in, profile, every cloud copy of your Stories with its history, your pictures and audiobook files, AI conversations, narration records, and AI and dictation usage counts. The few records we keep are listed under "How long we keep it" above. Stories saved only on your device stay there until you delete them.

Keeping it secure

  • All connections use encryption (HTTPS), and stored data is encrypted by our hosting provider.
  • Each account can reach only its own data; this is checked on our servers, not just in the app.
  • The keys to our paid services stay on our servers and never ship inside the app.
  • Sign-in tokens on phones are kept in the device's secure storage.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

You must be 18 or older to use WriteRiff. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has given us information, write to support@writeriff.com and we'll delete it.

Your rights and choices

We give everyone the same rights, wherever you live. You can ask us to:

  • See the personal information we hold about you, and get a copy;
  • Take it with you in a common, machine-readable format;
  • Correct it if it's wrong;
  • Delete it (you can also delete your account yourself, above);
  • Restrict or object to a particular use of it, where the law allows;
  • Withdraw consent you gave us at any time (this doesn't affect what we did before).

You can also export any Story yourself as a backup file from inside the app.

How to make a request. Write to support@writeriff.com, ideally from your account's email address, and say what you'd like. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours before we act, and we'll only use that information to check. We'll answer within one month (45 days where California or another US state's law applies), and tell you if we need more time and why, as the law allows. Requests are free. If we can't do what you ask, we'll explain why. You won't be treated differently for using your rights.

If European or UK data protection law applies (GDPR and UK GDPR)

WriteRiff is offered only in the United States during the beta. If European or UK data protection law applies to your information anyway (for example, because you're there while using WriteRiff), this section explains how we meet it.

Who is responsible. Beckman Studios LLC (doing business as WriteRiff), in the United States, is the "controller" of your personal information. Contact: support@writeriff.com. We're a small company and haven't appointed a data protection officer.

Why we're allowed to use your information (our "lawful bases"):

  • To provide the service you signed up for (performance of a contract): your account and sign-in, saving and syncing your Stories, the AI assistant, AI book covers, dictation and narration when you use them, and account emails.
  • Our legitimate interests, which we balance against your rights: keeping WriteRiff and your account secure, preventing abuse and illegal or harmful content (including automated safety filtering of AI requests), setting fair usage limits, keeping technical logs to fix problems, and keeping the record of an account deletion.
  • Your consent: optional emails, if we ever offer them. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligations: for example keeping spending records for our accounts, or answering lawful requests from authorities.

What you must give us. An email address and password are needed to create an account. Everything else, including the AI, dictation and narration features, is optional.

Where it comes from. Almost everything comes from you. If you use Sign in with Google, Google gives us the details listed above.

International transfers. We're based in the United States, and your information is stored there (Supabase, in Ohio) and processed by our US service providers (OpenAI, Google). When your information moves from the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland to the United States, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK's addendum for UK data) in our providers' data processing terms, or on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK and Swiss extensions where a provider is certified under it. You can ask us for more detail about these safeguards.

Complaints. You can complain to the data protection authority where you live or work, or, in the UK, to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). You can also complain to us first at support@writeriff.com; we'll answer within one month.

California (CCPA / CPRA)

This section uses the categories California law asks for.

  • What we've collected in the last 12 months: identifiers (email address, account number, IP address); customer records (your name, if you add a display name or use Sign in with Google); your content and audio (writing, pictures, AI conversations, voice recordings for dictation); internet and device information (request counts, device or browser type, sign-in records); and sensitive personal information limited to your account login (email address and password).
  • Where it comes from: from you, from your device, and from Google if you use Sign in with Google.
  • Why: the purposes in "How we use it" above.
  • Who we disclose it to, for business purposes: our service providers listed in "Who receives your information" (hosting, AI, speech and narration, email delivery), each only for the job described.
  • Selling and sharing: we don't sell your personal information or "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we haven't in the last 12 months. We have no actual knowledge of selling or sharing information of anyone under 16.
  • Sensitive information: we use your account login only to sign you in and keep your account secure, so there's nothing to limit.
  • How long we keep it: see "How long we keep it" above.
  • Your rights: to know what we collect, use and disclose; to get a copy; to delete; to correct; to opt out of sale or sharing (we don't do either); to limit use of sensitive information (we only use it as described); and not to be treated differently for using these rights. You can use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof that they act for you and ask you to confirm your identity with us.

Other US states

People in other US states with privacy laws (such as Colorado, Connecticut, Oregon, Texas and Virginia) have similar rights, and we give them to everyone. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer with "Appeal" in the subject. We'll respond within the time your state's law sets, and if you're still not satisfied you can contact your state's attorney general.

No automated decisions

We don't make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects based only on automated processing. We don't profile you for advertising.

Do Not Track

WriteRiff doesn't track you across other sites or apps, so it works the same way whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal.

Our website

writeriff.com shows information about WriteRiff and hosts the account pages (confirming your email, resetting a password and deleting an account). Those account pages send your request straight to our sign-in service and don't store anything themselves.

The website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which records visitors' IP addresses in its server logs for security. If we add visitor statistics, we'll use a privacy-friendly service that doesn't track you across sites (we plan to use Cloudflare Web Analytics), and we'll update this section first.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
