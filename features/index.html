Every tool a story needs, on one desk.

The craft of fiction hasn't changed: gather ideas, shape them, write, read, listen to good readers and revise. WriteRiff gives every step its own beautifully made tool, and keeps them all talking to each other.

Idea Vault

Ideas arrive whenever they like: in the shower, on a walk, halfway through someone else's book. The Idea Vault is your shoebox of notes, minus the chaos. Organize and keep track of every story idea, and find the right one the moment you need it.

Story Structure

Lay out your whole story the way writers have always loved to: scene cards on a board. Build your entire structure and plan in one spot, move things around until it clicks, and shape it to the way you work.

  • Customizable to your own method, not a fixed formula.
  • Clean and easy to use, so planning feels like play.
  • Your plan sits right next to the manuscript it shapes.

Folio, a word processor made for fiction

Folio is WriteRiff's own word processor, designed for fiction writers and story creation rather than office memos. Chapters, scenes and notes are where a novelist expects them, and the page stays calm so the words can take center stage.

Use Folio on its own, or together with the rest of WriteRiff.

Prefer your favorite word processor? Bring your manuscript in and take it out as a Word document or plain text.

Write anywhere

WriteRiff runs in any browser on your computer, and on your phone and tablet. It's designed for writers on the go, so you never lose a chance to record inspiration, in a place that inspires.

  • Your desk at home, a café table, the bus home.
  • Pick up exactly where you left off.

A book reader worthy of your book

There's nothing like seeing your story as a real book. WriteRiff's reader shows your manuscript just like any digital book you've bought: proper pages, comfortable type, your place remembered. When you're ready, share your book with the people you choose.

Critiques from readers and writers

Every good book has had good readers. Request critiques from readers and writers, critique their work in return, and keep the whole exchange organized. Critiques connect straight to your manuscript, so every comment sits beside the words it's about.

  • Ask for feedback when you're ready, from the readers and writers you choose.
  • Give back and grow as a reader too.
  • No more hunting through emails and margins.

Annotate as you read

Reading your own book is when the real edits jump out. Make notes on your manuscript right in the book reader, marking the changes you want to make, just like penciling in the margins of a proof copy. Then head back to Folio and make them.

Audiobooks of your own writing

Hear your story out loud. Create an audiobook of your writing, listen to it on a walk or a long drive, and share it with friends and family. Listening is also one of the best ways to catch a clunky sentence.

AI assistance, only if you want it

Let's be clear: WriteRiff's AI is not here to write your novel. It's a tool that helps you work through, organize and spark ideas, from the first glimmer of a premise to the finished version. You stay the author, always.

  2. Story development

All in one app, and entirely optional. Use it only when you want to; WriteRiff works just the same without it.
